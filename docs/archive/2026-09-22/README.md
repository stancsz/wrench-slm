scope to measurable productive value from the deterministic hybrid worker.

  contract, including deferred hardware and model-research gates.

  comparison contract.
- `WRENCH_MODEL_TIERS.md` is the former model-candidate comparison note.
- `COLLABORATION_CONTRACT.json` is the previous broad Q4 scope contract.
- `WRENCH_PORTABLE_DISTRIBUTION.md` is the former package distribution plan.
- `MODEL_ARTIFACT_TRANSFER.md` is the former cross-host artifact transfer plan.

They are retained for historical evidence and fixture provenance. They are not
active instructions. The active goal is the repository root `GOAL.md`.
