Wrench / Brand assets

A small mark.
A shared possibility.

The Open W pairs two interlocking forms in a compact, forward-leaning mark. It stands for Wrench and the idea of making more from smaller parts.

wrench

Built to work at small sizes

A simple silhouette, solid fills, and no fine detail. The same mark works in a navigation bar, a repository avatar, or a phone icon. The wordmark uses lowercase lettering for an approachable developer-tool identity.
