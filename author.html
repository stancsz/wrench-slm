A note from the author / Why this little wrench exists

For people
like me.

Hi, I’m the person behind Wrench.

I’m not an AI engineer at a big tech company. I’m an ordinary person with a limited budget and more ideas than it can comfortably fund. I want to build things with AI without mentally calculating the bill every time I try something new.

That’s why I started Wrench. I want to help bring the cost of using AI down, together. More people should be able to ask questions, experiment, and take a few wrong turns. Finding out an idea doesn’t work should be a useful lesson, not an expensive evening.

Spend the good tokens
on the hard parts.

Let small models and local tools do the legwork: reading files, finding things, turning the screws. Bring in a stronger model when the work needs deeper reasoning. I want to find out how much a modest GPU or an old phone can still contribute. Eventually, perhaps we can lend spare computing power to friends, too.

I’m also willing to provide technical support to model service providers who price transparently, work seriously on costs, and pass savings on to their users. I’d like to work with people who want to lower the barrier and make AI affordable to keep using. A tight budget deserves to be treated with care.

This project is still early, and there’s a long way to go. I’ll keep the distinction between what works and what we’re aiming for clear on the evidence page. Taking a look, asking a question, or pointing out something that doesn’t hold up can all help it move forward.

Let’s bring the cost of AI down together.
Curiosity shouldn’t have to check its balance every time.
